Travel and Expense Policy

1. General principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. Book travel through the company travel portal so that trips are covered by our travel insurance. Expenses that are not covered by this policy need written pre-approval from your manager.

2. Flights

Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or longer, premium economy is allowed. Business class requires approval from a vice president.

3. Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is $250 in most cities. In high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich and Singapore) the limit is $350 per night.

4. Meals

The daily meal allowance is $75 for domestic travel and $100 for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at approved team events.

5. Learning and conferences

Each employee has a learning budget of $2000 per calendar year for books, courses and conferences. Conference attendance also needs manager approval at least 4 weeks before the event.

6. Home office

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for equipment such as a desk, chair or monitor.

7. Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in PeopleHub within 30 days of the expense, with an itemized receipt for every item over $25. Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next payroll run.
