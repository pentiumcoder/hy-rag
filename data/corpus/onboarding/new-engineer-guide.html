New Engineer Guide

Welcome to Northwind! This guide covers your first six weeks. Your manager will assign you an onboarding buddy from your team on day one; ask them anything.

Week 1: Get set up

  • Collect your laptop and YubiKey from IT. Laptops arrive with full-disk encryption already enabled.
  • Request access to GitHub, Grafana and Beacon in the #it-help channel.
  • Install the internal CLI and run nwctl doctor. It checks your tools, credentials and VPN, and tells you how to fix anything missing.
  • Read the Engineering Handbook and the Architecture Overview.

Week 2: First change

Pick an issue labelled good-first-issue in your team's repository and ship it through the normal review process. Aim to have it in production via the release train by the end of the week.

Week 3: Shadow on-call

Shadow your team's primary on-call for a full week. You receive the same pages but are not responsible for responding. Join every incident call you can.

Weeks 4 to 6

Take on a small project with your buddy. At the end of week 6 you join the on-call rotation as a secondary.

Security training

Complete the security awareness training within your first 14 days. Production access cannot be requested until the training is complete.
