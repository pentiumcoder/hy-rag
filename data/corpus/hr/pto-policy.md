# Paid Time Off Policy

## Annual allowance

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, in addition to the public holidays of their country of employment. PTO accrues monthly, at roughly 2.08 days per month. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their contracted hours.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they expire. Unused days beyond the 5-day carryover limit are not paid out, except where local law requires it.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in PeopleHub. Requests longer than 3 consecutive working days must be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Engineers who are scheduled for on-call during the requested days must arrange a swap in Beacon before the request can be approved.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees have 10 paid sick days per year. No doctor's note is needed for absences of fewer than 3 consecutive days. Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

## Leaving Northwind

On your last day, accrued but unused PTO for the current year is paid out at your base salary rate.
