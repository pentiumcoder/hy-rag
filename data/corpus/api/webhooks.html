Webhooks

Webhooks notify your systems when something happens in your Northwind account. Configure endpoints under Settings → Webhooks; only Admins and Owners can manage them.

Event types

  • ingest.completed: a bulk import finished.
  • usage.threshold_reached: the account crossed 80% or 100% of its included monthly events.
  • invoice.created: a new invoice is available.
  • apikey.revoked: an API key was revoked.

Verifying signatures

Every delivery includes two headers:

  • X-NW-Timestamp: the Unix time at which the delivery was sent.
  • X-NW-Signature: an HMAC-SHA256 of the timestamp, a period, and the raw request body, keyed with your endpoint's signing secret, hex-encoded.

Compute the HMAC over the raw bytes before parsing the JSON, compare with a constant-time comparison, and reject deliveries whose timestamp is more than 5 minutes old to prevent replay attacks.

Delivery and retries

Your endpoint must return a 2xx status within 10 seconds. Any other result, including a timeout, counts as a failure. Failed deliveries are retried up to 8 times with exponential backoff, spread over roughly 24 hours. Deliveries can arrive more than once and out of order; use the id field to de-duplicate.

If every delivery to an endpoint fails for 3 consecutive days, the endpoint is disabled and the account Owners are emailed. Re-enable it from the dashboard after fixing the problem.

Testing

Use the "Send test event" button in the dashboard to deliver a sample ingest.completed event. Test deliveries are signed exactly like real ones.
