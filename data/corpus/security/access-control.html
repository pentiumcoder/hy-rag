Access Control Policy

This policy covers how customers control access to their Northwind accounts and how Northwind employees access production systems.

Customer roles

Every member of a customer account has exactly one role:

  • Viewer: can view dashboards and saved queries.
  • Analyst: everything a Viewer can do, plus running ad-hoc queries and creating dashboards.
  • Admin: everything an Analyst can do, plus managing API keys, members and webhooks.
  • Owner: everything an Admin can do, plus billing and deleting the account. Each account has at least one Owner.

Enterprise customers can enforce single sign-on with SAML and map identity-provider groups to roles.

Employee authentication

Employees sign in to all internal tools through Okta. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory, and access to production systems requires a hardware security key (YubiKey); SMS codes are not accepted.

Production access

No employee has standing access to production. Access is granted just in time:

nwctl access request --role prod-read --duration 4h --reason "INC-1234"

Requests are approved by the on-call lead of the owning team. The maximum duration is 8 hours; access then expires automatically. The prod-write role additionally requires an open incident or change ticket.

Break-glass access

If Okta or the access system itself is down, the break-glass account can be used. Using it requires approval from two members of the Security team. Every use is logged to the audit.breakglass stream, triggers a SEV3 ticket automatically, and is reviewed by Security within 24 hours.

Access reviews

Managers review their team's group memberships every quarter. Access for employees who leave Northwind is removed within 4 hours of their departure being recorded in PeopleHub.
