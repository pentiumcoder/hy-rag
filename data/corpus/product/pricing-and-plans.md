# Pricing and Plans

Northwind Cloud has three plans. Customers can upgrade at any time; downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

## Plan comparison

| | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $49 / month | $499 / month | Custom (annual contract) |
| Included events | 1 million / month | 50 million / month | Custom |
| API rate limit | 50 requests/second | 100 requests/second | 1000 requests/second |
| Data retention | 7 days | 30 days | 365 days |
| Support first response | 48 business hours | 8 business hours | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| Uptime SLA | none | 99.9% | 99.95% |
| SSO (SAML) | no | no | yes |
| Dedicated customer success manager | no | no | yes |

## Overages

Events above the included volume are billed at $0.20 per 100,000 events on Growth. Starter accounts that exceed their included events are throttled until the next billing period rather than billed. Enterprise overage pricing is set in the contract.

## Billing

Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and cover the previous month's usage. Payment is due within 30 days. Accounts with invoices more than 45 days overdue are suspended: ingestion stops, but data is retained for the rest of the plan's retention window.

## Trials

New accounts start with a 14-day Growth trial. No credit card is needed for the trial. At the end of the trial the account becomes Starter unless the customer chooses a paid plan.

## SLA credits

If monthly uptime falls below the plan's SLA, Growth and Enterprise customers can claim service credits: 10% of the monthly fee for each full 0.1% below the SLA, up to 50%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
